Why LSPU

A university built on quality, research, and service

Laguna State Polytechnic University is a state university dedicated to accessible, quality higher education across Laguna. Through its colleges, campuses, and research and extension programs, LSPU prepares graduates who are competitive, community-driven, and ready to serve.

Vision

An ASEAN Polytechnic University by 2030.

Mission

A premier ASEAN polytechnic university that LEADS in multidisciplinary education and applied research, equipping future-ready servant LEADers to drive innovation for sustainable regional development.

Quality Policy

LSPU delivers quality education through responsive instruction, distinctive research, sustainable extension and production services — continually improving to meet the university stakeholders' highest level of satisfaction with integrity, professionalism, and innovation.

Our Campuses

LSPU serves students across four campuses throughout Laguna, each offering its own mix of programs and community life.

Santa Cruz

The University's main campus, home to central administration and the widest range of academic programs.

Brgy. Bubukal, Santa Cruz, Laguna
(049) 304-7000

San Pablo City

Situated in the historic Seven Lakes City, offering strong programs in business, agriculture, and technology.

Del Remedio, San Pablo City, Laguna
(049) 554-9910

Los Baños

Located in a hub for science and research, with programs geared toward applied sciences and technology.

Malinta, Los Baños, Laguna
(049) 308-4530

Siniloan

Serving communities in eastern Laguna and neighboring Quezon Province with growing academic offerings.

L. de Leon St., Siniloan, Laguna
(049) 303-0030
