About LSPU

About LSPU

The university is governed by its Board of Regents and led by senior administrative officials overseeing multiple campuses. Its strategic plan focuses on sustainable growth and expanding academic offerings across diverse programs.

LSPU has consistently demonstrated operational efficiency and public service excellence, as evidenced by awards such as the Performance-Based Bonus (PBB) from the DBM, the Quality Management System Award from GQMP, the PRIME-HR Award from CSC, and the Platinum Award from IPOPHIL for intellectual property innovation.

Internationally, LSPU is steadily climbing university rankings, including EduRank, UniRank, Webometrics, AD Scientific Index, and Study Abroad Aide, reflecting strong research productivity, digital presence, and global competitiveness.

This chapter outlines LSPU's historical roots, governance, academic profile, and institutional standing, highlighting its continuous pursuit of quality education and innovation.

With a legacy spanning over 73 years, LSPU is a Level III State University and College holding ISO 9001:2015 and AACCUP accreditation.

Our Campuses

LSPU serves students across four campuses throughout Laguna, each offering its own mix of programs and community life.

Santa Cruz

The University's main campus, home to central administration and the widest range of academic programs.

Brgy. Bubukal, Santa Cruz, Laguna
(049) 304-7000

San Pablo City

Situated in the historic Seven Lakes City, offering strong programs in business, agriculture, and technology.

Del Remedio, San Pablo City, Laguna
(049) 554-9910

Los Baños

Located in a hub for science and research, with programs geared toward applied sciences and technology.

Malinta, Los Baños, Laguna
(049) 308-4530

Siniloan

Serving communities in eastern Laguna and neighboring Quezon Province with growing academic offerings.

L. de Leon St., Siniloan, Laguna
(049) 303-0030
